## 🎯 EASIEST METHOD: Netlify Drop (No CLI Needed!)

**No installation required - just drag & drop!**

1. Open your browser and go to: **https://app.netlify.com/drop**
2. Drag the `web-build` folder onto the page
3. Wait 30 seconds ⏱️
4. **Done!** You get a URL like: `https://amazing-app-xyz123.netlify.app`

**That's it!** Share the URL with anyone, anywhere in the world! 🌍
